# Top 10 research method — validation expansion

Owner-requested on November 2, 2026. Applies within the existing report lane and complements README.md.

## 1. Separate attention from Brief usefulness

Rank topics by observed attention, not personal usefulness. Every topic must retain an attention signal and confidence plus separate brief_priority, brief_fit and claim_verification fields. These are editorial judgments, not computed popularity scores. If comparable measurements are missing, disclose that the order is provisional and the precise rank is uncertain. Keep valuable low-attention leads in editorial_watchlist; do not suppress them from wider Brief discovery.

## 2. Make measurements comparable

Retain video ID, source URL, format, published_at, observed_at, source_metric_checked_at, precision and provenance. Record unavailable values as null. Retrieval time does not replace an unknown measurement time. Never compare a Short with a long video, add overlapping clusters, or derive growth from different trackers. Calculate growth only from the same video, format and source with distinct known measurement timestamps; disclose rounding. Views divided by age is a lifetime average, not current velocity. Channel-relative performance requires a measured channel baseline. Same-day rank movement caused by an expanded sample is a methodological revision, not evidence of rising or falling attention.

## 3. Validate claims and disclose coverage

Consult validation-resources.json alongside the existing source universe. Identify primary documents, independent reporting, original repository material, benchmark authors and background guidance separately. Record direct access versus indexed-only evidence, original publication date and the limited claim each source supports. Two pages from one author organization are not two independent confirmations. A source's existence does not verify every claim in it. Preserve disagreements and failed retrievals. Report metadata-screened videos, distinct channels, fully reviewed videos, direct counter verifications and transcripts separately. An unfinished 40–80 full-video review target remains incomplete even when more than 40 metadata entries were screened.

## 4. Reduce repetition and promotion

Cluster the same underlying event under one topic; distinguish infrastructure economics from the consumer allocation angle, local deployment from frontier comparison, and model rankings from task-specific agent evaluations. Never sum overlapping tracker clusters or treat syndication as independent breadth. Label vendor demos and unsupported earnings/superiority claims. Preserve the Brief's 2 Technical / 2 Applied / 2 Agents allocation, exactly one genuinely reusable Agent Skills procedure, two verified videos, two diverse podcasts and all existing novelty, freshness and original-source gates. Candidate priorities are search guidance, not automatic selections. A skills candidate must be checked for inputs, procedure, observable output and a meaningful validation check before selection.

## Reruns and audit

For an authorized same-cycle rerun, preserve the previous report/AAR in revisions/YYYY-MM-DD/ before updating today's canonical files with expected blob SHAs. Save actual report bytes, immutable commit/blob/SHA256 readback and a new run AAR. Preserve original observation timestamps. Never assert Compiler consumption or unattended execution without separate receipts. No new schedule, publisher, workflow or paid service is authorized by this method.
