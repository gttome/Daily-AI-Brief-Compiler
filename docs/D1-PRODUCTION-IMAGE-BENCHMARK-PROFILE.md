# External App Image Benchmark Profile

Updated: 2026-10-09. The historical D1 filename is retained for existing job links. This is a quality profile, not a requirement to use the retired D1 architecture or a Work chat.

## Current specification

Use [Perfect_Image_Specification.md](external-app/Perfect_Image_Specification.md), the [full app prompt](external-app/Brief_Compiler_Image_App_GitHub_Prompt.md), and [variation guidance](external-app/Image_Variation_After_Action.md). The existing app reads the prompt and GitHub, creates six images and returns the exact six PNGs plus the manifest/reviews. No specific chat platform is required.

## Visual quality

- Exact 1200 × 630 PNG, white or near-white, safe margins, crisp editorial linework and professional finish.
- Article-specific explanation with at least two distinctive source-supported anchors.
- At least 12 meaningful components, 3–5 major regions, two visible internal substages and at least two supported secondary evidence/constraint/provenance/feedback relationships.
- Approximately 80–90% useful explanatory occupancy without crowded labels or decorative padding.
- Clear hierarchy, visible transformations or comparisons, disciplined annotations and appropriate dimensional depth.
- Every factual component, arrow, number and label supported by the article or verified source evidence; conceptual geometry must not invent product behavior.

## Article-fit infographic styling is allowed

An infographic-like or hybrid presentation is permitted when it is the best explanation for the specific article: comparisons, timelines, taxonomies, source-supported quantitative relationships or structured explanatory panels. Two-dimensional clarity may be preferable to machinery. Keep the full factual, explanatory-detail, text, hierarchy and file standards. Generic sparse infographics, repeated card grids and decorative dashboards still fail; infographic style itself is not a defect.

For an infographic, count meaningful states, categories, supported measures and explanatory relationships, not repeated boxes or decorative icons. Internal substages can be a visible reasoning or comparison sequence. Do not force a physical machine, unsupported central engine or feedback loop into an article to satisfy a style checklist.

## Text is mandatory

Every image needs readable explanatory text, beyond a title. Prepare and freeze a nonempty source-supported exact label list. An empty legacy allowlist or labels_authorized=false means the app must complete that specification; it does not prohibit text. The owner authorizes the requirement and app-drafted wording, not a fabricated record of individual owner approval. Preserve immutable original job bytes and record the correction separately.

Prefer concise labels, at most eight words/80 characters each, with necessary units and qualifications. All required labels must appear clearly, without extra characters, pseudo-writing, placeholder paragraph lines, clipping or overlap. Necessary product names can appear as plain explanatory text; no logos or decorative branding.

## Six-image variation

Plan the set before generation, then generate and review one story at a time. Keep each generator's input story-only. Compare actual saved pixels and record visual signatures.

Targets: six composition signatures; at least four layouts, four diagram grammars, four hierarchy patterns, three annotation patterns, four dominant palette families and four mechanism metaphors. No more than two images share one primary grammar or a generic input–engine–output arrangement. Source faithfulness is never sacrificed to meet a count. Preserve accepted bytes through later failures.

## Acceptance

Actual saved-pixel inspection must establish article match, correct relationships and scope, readable labels, mechanism detail, hierarchy, professional finish and differentiation. Provide descriptive alt text. No people/humanoids, photorealism, logos, invented UI, generic AI symbols or low-quality fallback.

Inspect the exact committed PNGs after readback and bind each review to its image hash and story. Technical validity, a hash or an overall score is not visual acceptance. The historical October 3–7 review in the prompt is calibration evidence, not a set of automatically passing templates.

## Required correction and delivery loop

The executing session owns corrective work. Generate/edit, save, review actual pixels, record defects, correct and repeat full review until all six satisfy every applicable individual and set-level criterion. No fixed quality-attempt cap applies. Set-level failures require correction of the responsible images and renewed individual/set review. Preserve unaffected passing images; a necessary correction to an earlier accepted candidate is a versioned successor with old bytes and review history retained.

**The task is not finished until all six passing images are uploaded to GitHub, integrated into their correct articles in the dated Brief, published, and independently verified on the live site.** Uploads, a merged PR, a queued release, previews or a successful deployment status alone do not satisfy completion. Continue correcting image, integration and publication failures until the complete live result passes; a genuine unresolved access, tool or quota blocker leaves the task incomplete. Use the existing GitHub connection; verify exact GitHub readbacks and manifest/reviews before the protected image-only release, then verify all six live file hashes, article pairings and rendered pages. Report PUBLISHED_COMPLETE only after the full prompt's live-verification criteria pass. Genuine tool/access/quota/source blockers remain incomplete with a resume checkpoint.
