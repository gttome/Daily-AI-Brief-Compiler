## Scientific papers: selection policy

An original **research paper** is a technical study with a specific question, method, evidence/findings and stated limitations, accessible in the original manuscript or recognized proceedings. A vendor blog, press release, release note, generalized article, product benchmark announcement, or a citation to a paper without substantive review is *not by itself* a paper-led story.

At every genuine Compiler edition, research the actual enabled original-paper discovery sources already in `config/resource-registry.json`: arXiv cs.AI, cs.CL and cs.LG, ACL Anthology, OpenReview, ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS and the Hugging Face papers discovery feed. Discovery via a paper hub is not proof that its summary is an original scientific source. Do not bulk-enable disabled sources or treat an abstract preview as a full review.

Each candidate must be assessed for **original release date**, reproducible or inspectable research method, appropriate sample/baselines, concrete supported result, limitations/uncertainties, independent significance, direct paper link, publication status (**PREPRINT** vs **PEER_REVIEWED** with actual venue evidence), and a practical takeaway understandable to the intended audience. A peer-reviewed claim requires venue/publication evidence; an arXiv preprint alone is *not* peer reviewed. Apply all existing article quality, relevance, novelty, 2/2/2, factuality, independent publisher and freshness protections.

The **editorial target** is at least **one well-qualified paper-led article among the 42 stories published across seven consecutive editions**, preferably in a technical engineering slot; more are welcome when justified. The target is not a hard publisher gate, a forced weekly date, or permission to use an old paper without the required explicit fallback. If no eligible, novel and useful original paper is available, publish the best qualified six stories, record the sources examined and why paper leads failed, and make it a priority next edition.

Record edition-scoped `shadow-runs/E/editorial/scientific-paper-coverage.json` when real review is complete. Schema `daily-scientific-paper-coverage-v1`: `edition_date`, `review_status` (`VERIFIED` or `INCOMPLETE`), `published_papers` (array of `{story_id,title,original_paper_url,publication_status,research_limitations,review_scope}`). For genuine no-paper edition `VERIFIED` with an empty array means the actual final bundle was reviewed; an unknown or absent assessment remains `INCOMPLETE`/not recorded. Historical absence must not be invented. Use `validateScientificPaperEditionReceipt` against the exact bundle before declaring selection; the deterministic AAR reports self-attested matches separately.
