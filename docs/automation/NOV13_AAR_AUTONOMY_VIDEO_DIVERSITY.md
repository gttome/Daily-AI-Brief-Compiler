Canonical source identity comes from original publishing entity/channel, not hosting or relabeling. Review corrected published pages across the preceding seven editions.

For the next **three** new editions, actively seek fully substantive, non-promotional, archive-new candidates outside **CBS, Fox, PBS and TED**, emphasizing qualified independent research, practitioner/engineering, original conference, public-interest and educational publishers. Target **at least five different publisher families in a rolling 14-slot seven-edition window**, and **no family above four of fourteen**. Document the genuine original URL, original date, reviewed content scope, playback result, exact failure of each less-exposed alternative, and why the selected repeat is the most useful compliant program.
