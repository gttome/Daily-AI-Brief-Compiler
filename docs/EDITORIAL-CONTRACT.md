# Daily Compiler Editorial and Reader Contract

This document defines reader-facing output quality, not orchestration.

## Edition

Every edition must contain exactly:

- 6 article stories
- 2 Technical AI Engineering
- 2 Applied Generative AI for Knowledge Workers
- 2 Agents for Everyone
- exactly 1 reusable Agent Skills story
- 2 verified videos
- 2 source-diverse podcasts
- 1 refreshed Emerging AI Watchlist
- reader-value book connections covering the required Generative AI Professional Series
- 6 accepted professional explanatory diagrams

## Freshness

Prioritize the most recent 24 hours, use normal fallback through 72 hours, and use extended fallback through 168 hours only when necessary. Use original publication date rather than modification time.

## Story requirements

Each story requires: headline; original source date; authoritative/credible source link; professional explanatory diagram; substantive summary; Why it matters; reading-time estimate; focus/category; topics; coverage labels; related coverage; accessible image alt text; and a permanent dated story route.

## Media

Videos: exactly 2 verified videos, target <=10 minutes and fallback <=20 minutes only when necessary. Display title, focus, date, Duration, summary, Why it matters, and a source link opening in a new tab.

Podcasts: exactly 2 verified source-diverse episodes. Display episode title, source, original date, Duration, written reading-time estimate, summary, Why it matters, and a source link opening in a new tab.

## Emerging AI Watchlist

The edition must include New, Updated, and Carried forward states, what changed today, and dropped topics with a concise reason when applicable. The Watchlist must be meaningfully refreshed from current evidence.

## Books

Mappings are for reader value and must cover all four required titles across the edition:

- Reliable Generative AI
- Reliable Generative AI Context Engineering
- Generative AI Professional Prompt Engineering Guide
- Generative AI Prompt Engineering Learning Ecosystem

Every story must have at least one reader-value mapping. Do not expose production/run language.

## Images

Each story requires one 1200 x 630 professional explanatory diagram with a white/light background, strong hierarchy, readable labels, high detail, no overlapping text, no sparse generic boxes, no people/humanoids/faces/avatars/decorative characters, no low-quality fallback, and exact persisted-asset visual review before acceptance. Accepted images are immutable and never regenerated during recovery.

## Reader site

The deterministic reader projection must contain:

- homepage/latest view;
- dated edition page;
- six permanent dated story pages;
- dated Emerging AI Watchlist page;
- dated videos page;
- dated podcasts page;
- archive navigation;
- Earlier Brief links;
- reading time;
- source publication date;
- topics and coverage labels;
- related coverage;
- five-star usefulness rating interface;
- share functionality;
- share count where supported;
- accessible image alt text;
- mobile-readable presentation;
- external media/source links opening in a new tab;
- JSON feed and RSS feed.

Do not include removed elements such as “Original Commentary” or “What do stars mean”. Do not expose task IDs, run names, execution IDs, migration keys, or orchestration tokens in reader-facing content.

## Permanent history

A later deployment must preserve earlier dated edition routes, story routes, media routes, Watchlist routes, and assets. The deterministic compiler rebuilds the shadow site from all verified shadow editions plus the currently compiling edition so permanent routes survive future deployments.

## Simplicity invariant

Reader quality may not be reduced to simplify production. Reader requirements also may not be used to reintroduce a Supervisor, Watchdog Ring, writer/recovery leases, worker pools, continuous AI polling, wake PRs, 30-task orchestration, or runtime software-repair loops.
