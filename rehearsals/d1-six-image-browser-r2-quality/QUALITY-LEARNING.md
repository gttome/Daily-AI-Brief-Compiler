# R2 quality repair learning

R1 proved the browser/image path but exhausted four genuine Story 1 attempts without acceptance. No asset was accepted, so R2 does not redo accepted work.

The repeated defects were:
- placeholder/pseudotext-like horizontal marks on blank evidence/document surfaces;
- generic stacked-sheet and floating-panel motifs;
- processing/review geometry that looked decorative rather than causal;
- polished workflow-infographic appearance below the required high-density textbook mechanism benchmark.

R2 changes prompt specificity, not the architecture:
- exposed internal causal substructure in Process;
- explicit multi-fragment evidence lattice with trace binding;
- mechanistic multi-criterion Review gate;
- specific return junction and re-entry point;
- explicit ban on generic sheet stacks, placeholder lines, dashboard panels, and decorative geometry.

The quality threshold is not weakened.

## Observed R2 outcome — 2026-10-07

Four genuine Story-1 candidates were generated and reviewed in a fresh regular story chat. All four were rejected by that chat. Candidate 1 gained causal/internal mechanism structure but failed text cleanliness and non-photorealism as well as premium/decorative-geometry checks. Candidates 2–4 passed the text, non-photorealism, causal-density, differentiated-fragment/trace, and pass/return checks. The final two remaining failed criteria were `premium_textbook_editorial_quality` and `no_decorative_internal_geometry`.

The final reviewer cited symbolic process elements, partially abstract fragment-to-criterion validation mappings, simplified review comparison geometry, and ornamental repeated/connector elements. These are reviewer observations, not Work's independent visual judgments. The quality threshold was preserved, no fifth attempt was consumed, and no accepted image exists. The next action under the unchanged sealed plan is blocked; no architecture or sealed-prompt redesign was performed.
