Summary: Google Workspace is rolling out reusable skills that encode team rules, templates, and reference files across Studio, Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive, and Chat. Teams can create, test, share, and audit copies, then invoke them manually or automatically. Sheets and Meet are excluded, English is required, and shared Drive permissions still apply.

Why it matters: Build one bounded skill from an approved template and reference file, test it on known examples, and share a copy with one teammate. Validate output, permissions, audit events, and update ownership before enabling automatic invocation.
