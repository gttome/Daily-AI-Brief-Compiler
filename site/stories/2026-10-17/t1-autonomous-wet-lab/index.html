Summary: Google Research describes VERID, a semantic digital twin that tracks robot geometry, well volumes, reagents and pipette inventory before an AI agent executes laboratory protocols. Virtual dry runs and hardware checks reject impossible commands, while synchronized video and action logs support debugging. Separate closed-loop experiments improved aptamer selection, but physical readouts and human scientific direction remain essential.

Why it matters: Engineering teams can borrow the pattern without operating a lab: represent live system state, simulate proposed actions, block invalid commands and retain synchronized evidence. Evaluate recovery from depleted resources and hardware failures before granting an agent authority over physical or costly workflows.
