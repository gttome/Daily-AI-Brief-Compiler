Summary: OpenAI says its Performance Factory investigated 1,700 slow-session reports and identified more than 600 root causes in just over three months. Engineers manually reviewed 100 generated suggestions: 70% were accurate while 30% needed refinement. The workflow adds an adversarial reviewer, engineer approval and post-deployment recovery checks before treating a diagnosis as resolved.

Why it matters: Reliability teams can separate detection from proof of repair. Sample generated diagnoses, record reviewer corrections, require an owner before merging changes and verify that the affected performance distribution recovers after deployment instead of closing incidents from plausible explanations alone.
