The release offers instruction and reasoning modes, self-hosting options, model artifacts and a modified open-weight license. Benchmark leadership and efficiency claims are publisher-reported and still require workload-specific evaluation.

Why it matters: Engineering teams can evaluate the downloadable model against one representative coding or defensive-security task, recording hardware needs, token use, quality, license constraints and failure modes before choosing hosted or self-managed deployment decisions.
