Summary: Google says Gemini in Slides can update multiple slides or an entire presentation from one prompt. Users can change visual styles, revise content with Drive references, combine slides or normalize formatting. The result remains a native editable deck, and a change summary, undo and version history support review. Launch support is English-only.

Why it matters: Presentation owners can test one bounded deck-wide change, inspect every affected slide and compare version history before accepting it, reducing repetitive formatting work without treating a single prompt as final editorial approval.
