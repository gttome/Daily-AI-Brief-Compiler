Summary: Plaud’s Skills feature saves reusable instruction sets that can be created from templates, conversations, files, forms or artifacts, then invoked automatically in Work mode. Users review a draft before saving, and the platform limits each workspace to 100 skills. Usage consumes Plaud Credits, so repeatability still needs cost and quality checks.

Why it matters: Create one meeting-decision skill that extracts decisions, owners, deadlines and open questions with transcript evidence. Test it on three varied recordings, compare every claim with the source, and require human approval before saving the skill or sharing its brief.
