Summary: Zoom’s Unified Context Layer is designed to connect meeting, chat, document and third-party records so people and agents can retrieve the thread behind a decision. Announced capabilities include Search & Ask APIs, configurable access and My Notes workflows. Availability varies by feature, and productivity claims are not independent outcome evidence.

Why it matters: Before adopting a conversation layer, inventory which records may be joined, who can retrieve them and how corrections propagate. Pilot one meeting-to-action workflow with permission checks, source links and human review instead of granting broad cross-system context.
