Summary: Alibaba Cloud opened Qwen4-Max-Preview from November 11 through November 25 for eligible Token Plan users. The temporary model supports a one-million-token context window, multimodal inputs, deep reasoning and tools, but has no pay-as-you-go option and will be retired after the preview. The notice establishes access and limits, not independent quality or cost advantages.

Why it matters: Use the preview for one long-document task with known answers. Log retrieved passages, tool calls, corrections, latency, and token consumption, then export prompts and results before retirement so the evaluation survives the temporary endpoint.
