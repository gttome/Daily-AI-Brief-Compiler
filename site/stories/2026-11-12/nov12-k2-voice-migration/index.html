Summary: OpenAI will retire ChatGPT Advanced Voice and Standard Voice on December 11, making GPT-Live the default voice experience. Existing conversation history remains, while live video and screen sharing temporarily continue through Advanced Voice until GPT-Live supports them. The notice describes a product transition; it does not establish that every voice workflow will retain equivalent behavior.

Why it matters: Inventory voice workflows before December 11. Test turn-taking, accents, interruptions, tool use, history, and any video or screen-sharing dependency, then document a fallback for tasks whose review or accessibility requirements change under GPT-Live.
