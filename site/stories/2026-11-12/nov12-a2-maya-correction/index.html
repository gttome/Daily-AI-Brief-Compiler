Summary: Pietro Miotti’s agent workflow flagged a Maya site in public LiDAR data, but a November 11 correction says researchers had already mapped it in a 2025 paper. The site was absent from the Mexican heritage register the workflow checked. The episode illustrates rediscovery, not archaeological discovery, and the author explicitly says he is not an archaeologist.

Why it matters: Before announcing an agent’s discovery, search domain literature, contact qualified experts, compare official registries with publication databases, and label unresolved identity questions. Preserve corrections so the workflow rewards verified novelty, not merely a missing database entry.
