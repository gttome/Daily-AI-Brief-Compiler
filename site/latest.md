### Tech development pushback sways elections

<a href="{{ '/videos/2026-11-08/general/' | relative_url }}" data-item-id="dab-video-2026-11-08-general" data-edition-date="2026-11-08" data-action="permanent_page_clicks">Open the permanent video page</a>
