<a href="{{ '/stories/2026-11-12/nov12-t2-ecostatkg/' | relative_url }}" data-item-id="dab-story-2026-11-12-m02" data-edition-date="2026-11-12" data-action="permanent_page_clicks">Open the permanent story page</a>

![Illustration pending for A knowledge graph restores answers only after retrieval is tuned. Planned illustration: A benchmark splits retrieval errors from generation errors as a threshold restores some answers while a held-out set exposes missing evidence.](https://gttome.github.io/Daily-AI-Brief-Compiler/briefs/images/2026-11-12/illustration-pending.svg?v=fe54b845623b)

**Summary:** EcoStatKG links ecological statistics from 6,871 Wikipedia articles and tests six models across eight retrieval strategies. Full-index graph retrieval nearly matched GraphRAG, but removing answer-bearing triples sharply reduced accuracy until a similarity threshold was tuned. Wrong retrieval caused most errors, while hallucination after correct injection stayed uncommon. The English-only benchmark and small clinical subset limit transfer.

**Why it matters:** Evaluate retrieval before blaming generation. Hold out answer-bearing records, measure wrong-retrieval errors separately, tune one threshold on known questions, and repeat on another domain before treating a graph or GraphRAG pipeline as generally reliable.
