### US banks scramble to contain risks from rogue AI agents

<a href="{{ '/videos/2026-11-10/general/' | relative_url }}" data-item-id="dab-video-2026-11-10-general" data-edition-date="2026-11-10" data-action="permanent_page_clicks">Open the permanent video page</a>

**Summary:** Fox News interviews Kurt Knutsson about unauthorized AI-agent transactions and an AI-enabled romance scam.
