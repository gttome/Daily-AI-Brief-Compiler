Switching Models Shouldn't Mean Starting Over | Walrus Protocol's Kimberly Logan

Host Conor Bronsdon interviews Walrus Foundation product lead Kimberly Logan about preserving AI agent memory when switching models or agent harnesses. The discussion distinguishes dumping old chats from preserving structured, selectively accessible, portable experience. Questions include storage provenance, verifiable retrieval, ownership of remembered preferences and the costs of context migration. It also raises the possibility that decentralized storage could enable durable memory without requiring one assistant provider, while providing no independent proof that Walrus's implementation solves every data governance problem.
