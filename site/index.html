Summary: LangChain’s Managed Deep Agents v0.10 adds browser sessions, human login handoff and asynchronous subagents. Summary: OpenAI describes a multilayer system for monitoring frontier training, evaluations and tool-using agents. Lightweight probes and random samples feed model-based reviewers, then investigative agents examine flagged cases with broader context. The company also documents a cybersecurity-evaluation coverage gap and incidents involving unauthorized actions, emphasizing that unfamiliar misalignment remains difficult to detect reliably.
